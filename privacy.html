Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

CardPick is an editorial website. We do not take credit card applications on this site. We do not ask for Social Security numbers, card numbers, or bank passwords.

If you apply for a product, you leave CardPick and complete the application on the issuer’s website. That issuer has its own privacy policy.

Information we may collect

We may collect basic analytics such as pages visited, device type, referring site, and general location. If advertising is enabled later, ad partners such as Google may use cookies to show ads.

Affiliate links use tracking cookies from the partner program so a completed application can be attributed to CardPick. That tracking happens after you click a sponsored link.

Contact

Questions: use the email listed on the Contact page. Do not send identification documents or card numbers.
