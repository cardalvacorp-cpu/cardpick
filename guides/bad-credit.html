Cards for fair or poor credit

Affiliate disclosure. We may earn a commission if you apply and get approved. Nobody can promise approval. This is not credit counseling.

Be blunt with yourself

Sapphire, Gold, and other premium cards almost never approve damaged or thin files. Applying anyway costs you a hard pull. A secured card is the grown-up first step.

You put down a deposit. That deposit becomes your limit. If you pay on time, the deposit is not a fee — it is collateral. After a stretch of clean use, some issuers review you for an unsecured card.

What to look for

Reports to all three bureaus. A deposit you can actually afford. No junk “credit repair” add-ons. Discover it Secured is a common path because it reports and can earn cash back. OpenSky exists for people other issuers decline. It is a tool, not a rewards product.

Credit Karma is not a card. Use it to see a score estimate before you apply.

Rules that matter more than the plastic

Stay under about 30% of the limit. Pay the statement in full. Do not open three secured cards in the same month. Wait. Rebuilding is slow on purpose.
