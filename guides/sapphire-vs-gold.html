Chase Sapphire Preferred vs. Amex Gold

Affiliate disclosure. Commission possible if you apply and get approved. Not financial advice.

Sapphire usually costs less per year and is built for travel points. Gold costs more and pays off if you actually use the dining and grocery credits.

If you are not sure you will use those credits every month, skip Gold. Visa from Chase is accepted in more places than Amex.
