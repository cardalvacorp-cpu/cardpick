What “no annual fee” actually means

The issuer does not charge you a yearly fee just to keep the account open. You can still pay interest if you carry a balance. You can still pay late fees. A $0 annual fee is not a free loan.

If you are still building credit, or you will not use lounge access and travel credits, start here. Paying $95 or $325 a year only makes sense when you will use those extras.

Three honest lanes

Rotating categories. Discover it Cash Back typically runs a 5% category you must activate, plus 1% on everything else. Miss the activation and you leave money on the table. First-year match offers exist in some periods — confirm the live terms.

Flat cash back. Capital One Quicksilver is for people who will not track categories. A steady 1.5% on every purchase is easier to keep.

Points that stay in a family. Chase Freedom Unlimited has no annual fee. The points matter more if you later add a Sapphire card and transfer them. If you will never do that, a simple cash-back card is cleaner.

How to choose in one minute

Do you refuse to think about categories? Quicksilver. Will you activate a category each quarter? Discover it. Do you already plan to live in Chase Ultimate Rewards? Freedom Unlimited. None of those paths guarantees approval.

Check your score first. A hard pull for a card you will not get still sits on your file.
